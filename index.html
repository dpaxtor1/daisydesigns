How it works

Two consultations, so your site sounds like you and nothing gets lost along the way.

  1. 01

    Project form

    Tell me about your business in a short online form. I’ll reach out within 2 business days to talk it through.

  2. 02

    Planning consultation

    We talk about your customers, goals, colors and content, and set up your accounts together, with you typing your own passwords. You receive a fixed quote, and the down payment books your spot.

  3. 03

    Design & build

    I design your site and build a private preview you can open on your phone.

  4. 04

    Review consultation

    We walk through the preview together. Your build includes two rounds of revisions: you send me one list of changes, I update the site, and then we do it once more.

  5. 05

    Launch & hand-off

    I test everything, take your site live, and you pay the final balance. Then I make you an Owner of your website on GitHub, so it’s officially yours.

After launch: maintenance

Totally optional. Sites on GitHub Pages have no software to update, so they don’t break on their own. Maintenance just means keeping your info current. Pick whichever fits how often your business changes.

Questions

Who owns my website?

You do. We set up your accounts together, in your name and with your email, and you create your own passwords. Your website lives in its own GitHub organization, and at launch I make you an Owner of it. I stay on only as a helper, and I never need your passwords.

How long does it take?

Often about a week from our planning consultation to launch. It can take longer if we schedule a photo session, or while I wait on answers, text or photos from you.

What do I need to get started?

Just the project form. If you have a logo, photos or menu, great. If not, we’ll figure it out together in our planning consultation.

Can I update the site myself?

Things like your Shopify products, booking app and Google profile, yes. Each has an easy dashboard, and I’ll show you how. Changes to the website itself are quickest through me.

Can customers pay me through the site?

Yes, through trusted services like Payhip, Shopify, Square or Toast. They handle the cards securely, so your customers’ details never touch your website.
